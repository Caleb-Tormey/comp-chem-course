18.1 Problem 1: Letting the Computer do the Calculus

In Chapter 2, we established the Expectation Value equation for the Variational Method: \[ E_{guess} = \frac{\langle \psi | \hat{H} | \psi \rangle}{\langle \psi | \psi \rangle} \]

We want to find the ground state energy of the 1D Harmonic Oscillator. We will work in Atomic Units so we can ignore mass and Planck’s constant (\(m=1, \hbar=1, k=1\)). * Our Hamiltonian is: \(\hat{H} = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{d^2}{dx^2} + \frac{1}{2}x^2\) * Our Ansatz (Guess) is a single Gaussian: \(\psi = e^{-\alpha x^2}\)

18.3 Problem 3: The Anharmonic Oscillator
