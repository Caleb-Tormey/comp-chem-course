17.3 Problem 3: The Michaelis-Menten Nightmare

In biochemistry, enzymes follow Michaelis-Menten kinetics. The reaction velocity (\(v\)) depends on the substrate concentration (\([S]\)):

\[v = \frac{V_{max} [S]}{K_M + [S]}\]

This equation is notoriously difficult to fit. If your initial guess for \(K_M\) is negative, the denominator might become zero during the optimization loop, and the algorithm will crash!
